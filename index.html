Questions finance teams ask.

Is there a monthly fee or minimum?

No. Pricing is pay-as-you-go per transaction, with no monthly minimum and no custody fees. Volume and OTC pricing kicks in automatically as your flow grows.

How is the settlement rate set?

You settle to UCoin, a dollar-pegged balance (1 UCoin ≈ $1.00). We don’t bake a hidden spread into that rate, the per-transaction fee is the whole cost.

Which currencies and rails do you support?

USDT and USDC, and local bank transfers, settling across 20+ currencies with payouts reachable in 100+ countries. More rails are added over time.

How fast do payouts arrive?

On-chain payouts settle within minutes once you approve the batch. Bank cash-outs follow the destination rail’s timing (often same-day for supported corridors).

Do you custody our funds or keys?

You authorize every payout by signing with your own wallet or passkey, VVBusiness never holds your key, and a code alone never releases funds.

How long is customer data retained?
